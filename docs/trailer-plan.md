# Ground Truth: 30-second trailer plan

Status: plan for review (updated 2026-11-15 to match the pitch deck's "real trends, not noise" story). Nothing generated or recorded yet.

## The rules we must meet (NASA Space Apps project submission guide)
- No longer than 30 seconds.
- Hosted outside the site (YouTube or Google Drive) with a public link.
- No copyrighted material we don't have permission for (music, images). So the music is generated or properly licensed, and no NASA logo is used as if we were NASA. We say "built with NASA data".
- English subtitles if anything is spoken in another language. We burn in English captions anyway, because judges often watch muted.

Source: [Space Apps project submission guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)

## The idea
A movie trailer, not a demo. It opens like a disaster film (the planet is changing and nobody knows what's real), the beat drops on the title, then the product appears as the hero, and it ends on a line that makes you want to try it.

**Logline:** *Every satellite sees Earth changing. Ground Truth tells you which changes are real.*

**Look:** dark space, real satellite imagery, the yellow highlighter as the signature (it "marks the truth"), and Barlow Condensed titles slamming in.

**Sound:** a low drone and ticking, a riser, a hard hit and silence on the title, then a driving pulse under the product shots, and a final boom on the end card.
